Building footprint data

Contains computer-generated building footprints from Microsoft US Building Footprints, served by Esri's MSBFP2 feature layer. Microsoft makes this dataset available under the Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. The adapted footprint database is made available under ODbL 1.0. This is the US Building Footprints dataset, not the separately licensed Global ML dataset.

  • Original nine-feature WGS84 extract (GeoJSON)
  • Adapted footprint geometry and 2023 surface statistics (EPSG:7131 GeoJSON)
  • Runtime scene database, including transformed footprint geometry and surface-derived patches (JSON)
  • Original extract attribution and processing notes

The imagery-derived outlines have uncertain dates and are not surveyed as-built footprints; they may merge or omit structures. Cellhouse identity is based on location and is tentative.

Elevation sources and interpretation

Terrain is derived from the USGS CA San Francisco B23 2023 bare-earth elevation data. The roof visualization uses the USGS 2023 maximum-surface 2 m raster. See the USGS project metadata and surface data metadata. Local horizontal coordinates use EPSG:7131 origin E 50375, N 32450; heights use NAVD88 metres and GEOID18, with no vertical exaggeration. NAVD88 zero is not local mean sea level.

Water exclusion is inferred from a hydro-flattened value, not a surveyed shoreline or seabed. Ground beneath buildings may be interpolated. Roof patches summarize surface measurements and can contain vegetation or equipment; thresholds, flat steps, wall heights and gaps are approximations, not verified architecture. This current-era experimental preview is not for navigation or safety use.
