Terrain and surface elevations

USGS CA San Francisco B23 2023 bare-earth DEM and 2 m maximum-surface measurements. Project metadata · surface data metadata. EPSG:7131 origin E 50375, N 32450; y is NAVD88 metres, GEOID18, 1:1 scale. Inferred water and unknown samples are not measured land or seabed. The lower flanks and −15 m NAVD88 plain are fictional gameplay terrain, not a bathymetric survey; a USGS regional report gives 12–14 m MLLW depths roughly 0.3 mile northeast, which is context rather than a local measurement. The 6 m road and local cut/fill are fictional, not a surveyed or certified route. A fictional entry apron and connecting path add local grading and paving. Measured source land remains unchanged outside the documented bounded road, apron and path zones. The fictional painted footpath between apron and road changes surface appearance but not elevation. Detailed terrain and road metadata.

Building outlines are imagery-derived, can merge or omit structures, and are not surveyed walls. Closed blocks are approximations, with buried foundations only to avoid visual gaps. Two structures include coherent interior 2 m surface regions filled down to their lower median roof plane; stairs and flat patches are simplifications, not measured architectural roof geometry. The small object 9608170 uses the whole-footprint surface median despite high edge-contamination risk. The likely water tower is a round tank on open, cross-braced legs; its support and tank proportions are illustrative, not surveyed. Maximum-surface measurements may include vegetation or equipment. Cellhouse and tower identifications are location-based and qualified.

Reference-led architecture

The cellhouse exterior uses visual cues from 1986 HABS views of the south front, east side, and roof and skylights. The window bands on probable New Industries are informed by the NPS view; probable Model Industries uses a separately identified HABS view. Opening counts, dimensions, three-level treatment, approximated steps and skylight placement are interpreted, not surveyed or established as exact 1962 conditions; the door is closed and no interior is implemented. Photos are references only and are not included as textures.
