Return to game blockout

This early exterior game blockout is not an architectural survey. It has walking and exploration, with a playable, provisional enclosed entrance vestibule; it is not a complete 1962 reconstruction and does not yet have supported underwater terrain.

Terrain and surface elevations

USGS CA San Francisco B23 2023 bare-earth DEM and 2 m maximum-surface measurements. Project metadata · surface data metadata. EPSG:7131 origin E 50375, N 32450; y is NAVD88 metres, GEOID18, 1:1 scale. Inferred water and unknown samples are not measured land or seabed. The lower flanks and −15 m NAVD88 plain are fictional gameplay terrain, not a bathymetric survey; a USGS regional report gives 12–14 m MLLW depths roughly 0.3 mile northeast, which is context rather than a local measurement. The shortened 6 m game road and local cut/fill are fictional, not a surveyed or certified route; some segments are steep. A fictional entry apron and connecting path add local grading and paving. Measured source land remains unchanged outside the documented bounded road, apron and path zones. The fictional painted footpath between apron and road changes surface appearance but not elevation. Detailed terrain and road metadata.

Building outlines are imagery-derived, can merge or omit structures, and are not surveyed walls. Closed blocks are approximations, with buried foundations only to avoid visual gaps. Two structures include coherent interior 2 m surface regions filled down to their lower median roof plane; stairs and flat patches are simplifications, not measured architectural roof geometry. The small object 9608170 uses the whole-footprint surface median despite high edge-contamination risk. The likely water tower is a round tank on open, cross-braced legs; its support and tank proportions are illustrative, not surveyed. Maximum-surface measurements may include vegetation or equipment. Cellhouse and tower identifications are location-based and qualified.

Reference-led architecture

The cellhouse exterior uses visual cues from 1986 HABS views of the south front, east side, and roof and skylights. The window bands on probable New Industries are informed by the NPS view; probable Model Industries uses a separately identified HABS view. Opening counts, dimensions, three-level treatment, approximated steps and skylight placement are interpreted, not surveyed or established as exact 1962 conditions; the operable door and enclosed 4.2 × 4.5 m vestibule are gameplay approximations, not a verified plan; the rear wall closes the space and no cellblock is modeled. Photos are references only and are not included as textures.

Lighthouse and Warden’s House

The lighthouse is placed using an approximate U.S. Coast Guard position. Its 4.191 m base and 27.489 m base-to-ventilator-ball model follow a 1910 light-station inventory. NPS and USCG descriptions give different heights; the lantern, rail and windows are approximations. The adjacent Warden’s House is a visual interpretation of the intact prison-era building, using a 1954 image and National Park Service history; the real house burned in 1970. Its 20 × 12 m footprint, roof, veranda, windows and details are inferred, not surveyed; no interior is modeled. The position uses a mapped point: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. The historical keeper’s quarters attached to the lighthouse are a separate building and are not yet modeled. Sources and limitations.

Original surface textures

The concrete, painted-steel door and sky-toned window-glass textures were procedurally generated for this blockout; they use no source photographs or external image assets. The opaque glass has a static reflection-like tint, not a live environmental reflection. Door surface and hardware are visual approximations, not verified 1962 details.

Human sample

The static adult model was generated with MPFB 2.0.18 and Blender. The MPFB core base mesh and targets are CC0; the simple opaque clothing and materials were generated for this sample. It is an approximate, unanimated visual test, not a historical uniform or finished character. MPFB licensing information · Sample provenance.
